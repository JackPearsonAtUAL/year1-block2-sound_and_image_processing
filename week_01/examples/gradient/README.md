# Week 1: Pixels and Colour

## Controls

LEFT (arrow) cycles left through the gradients
RGHT (arrow) cycles right through the gradients
UP (arrow) makes gradient go along the Y axis
DOWN (arrow) makes the gradient go along the X axis

## Personal Thoughts and Reflection
I want to preface this by having it known that I initially misunderstood the assignment brief for this week. I thought it said that I needed to find 3 - 5 colour palettes and create a gradient to go with each one, hence why there are four entirely different gradients & palettes.  

Out of the five palettes and paired gradients, I am most satisfied with numbers one (magenta -> cyan) and three(yellow -> orange). I find that the dark purples work very well with the brightly saturated cyan and magenta. This is also the gradient which I had the most trouble trying to get looking right. WHen re-reading the brief, I realised my mistake and chose to do the gradient variations based on the first palette. These variations include:

    #ff0000 -> #0000ff
    #ff1b6b -> #45caff
    #5a2641 -> #2d8db4
    #ff80ac -> #ade8ff
    #f42536 -> #5190f6  

Variation one is the gradient initially used in the example project. However I'm not a fan on any of as pure colours, since I find them quite unpleasant to look at. The second variation is the one I chose to settle on, as I find the contrast with the palette the best. Whereas with the third variation, the gradient is darker and blends more with the palette. While I do like this I don't think it is suitable for something like a poster, as it would be more difficult to read the text. Finally with variation four, I think that it would work best for the poster, as a paler background would keep costs down and make the text more readable.  With the final variation, I wanted to make a sort of middle ground between the first and third gradients, to see wheter I liked the colours more. While I do find them easier to look at, I'm still not the biggest fan. I still find he red too saturated, however I do like the darker cyan colour. However, I still like the second variation the most and have chosen to stay with it.
